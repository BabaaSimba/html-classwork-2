Tekhub Student Handbook

Welcome to the official Tekhub Student Handbook. This guide outlines everything you need to succeed throughout your technical training program, ensuring clear standards for conduct, academics, and communication.

Rules and Expectations

We maintain a professional and collaborative learning environment. Adherence to our core rules ensures that every student gets the absolute maximum value out of their time.

Attendance

Consistent attendance is mandatory for all classes, labs, and workshops. Missing sessions disrupts team collaboration and slows down your overall technical progress

Lateness

Arriving on time shows respect for your instructors and fellow students. Habitual lateness impacts your participation score and professional evaluation

Absense

If you are unable to attend due to illness or emergency, you must notify administration ahead of time and catch up on missed coursework promptly.

Devices

Students must bring a fully charged laptop and required tools to every session. Distracting usage of personal electronics during lectures is strictly prohibited.

Conduct

Mutual respect, constructive feedback, and ethical behavior form the bedrock of our community. Harassment or disruptive behavior will face disciplinary action.

Courses

Our curriculum is structured into intensive, industry-aligned tracks designed to take you from absolute beginner to job-ready software developer.

Frontend Development

Learn how to craft responsive, accessible, and dynamic user interfaces using modern web standards and client-side scripting frameworks.

Weeks 1 to 4: HTML

Master semantic elements, document structuring, accessibility principles, links, tables, and forms to build the structural skeleton of the web.

Weeks 5 to 8: CSS

Style your web pages using Flexbox, CSS Grid, responsive media queries, and modern visual design systems to create stunning layouts

backend Development

Dive deep into server-side logic, databases, API architecture, authentication security, and server deployment protocols.

Contact

If you have any questions or require administrative assistance, reach out to our support staff via our main portal or visit the front desk during office hours.
