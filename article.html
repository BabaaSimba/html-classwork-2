The Kogi state Police has arrested eight cultists and 3 atm fraudsters


October 21, 2026 4:34 pm

By Gbenga Odugun



Security operatives in Kogi State have apprehended eight individuals linked to cult activities in Itobe, Ofu LGA, while separately nabbing a three-man crew running an ATM card-swapping scam in Idah.


The details of the raid were made public by the state's Commissioner of Police, Bello Kankarofi,during a press briefing at the command headquarters in Lokoja."


On October 17, our division in Itobe worked closely with local security teams and community leaders to raid a hideout behind a secondary school. We apprehended eight suspects—including a minor—and recovered weapons like an axe and a cutlass as they gathered to choose a new cult leader.

CP Bello Kankarofi, Kogi State Commissioner of Police

The Kogi State Police Command has arrested eight people for suspected cultism in Itobe, and three men for an ATM card-swapping scam in Idah.


According to the State Commissioner of Police, CP Bello Kankarofi, police and local security teams raided a location behind a secondary school in Itobe on October 17, 2026


They caught eight males—including a 14-year-old—who had gathered to choose a new cult leader. The police recovered two motorcycles, an axe, and a cutlass from them. Separately, in Idah, officers tracked down three suspects (Philip Akiola, Ojo Thomas, and Abdulrauf Suleiman) who ran a fraud ring that stole 1.5 million Naira from a victim's bank account through a card-swapping trick.


Police found 59 ATM cards from different banks and a getaway vehicle during their arrest. Both cases are now being handled by the authorities for further investigation.
