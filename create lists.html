1. Jollof Rice Recipe

Ingredients

  • For the base
    • Fresh plum tomatoes
    • Rojo/Tatashe peppers
    • Scotch bonnet peppers (Ata rodo)
    • Onions and garlic
  • For the rice
    • Long grain parboiled rice
    • Vegetable oil
    • Tomato paste
    • Chicken stock and spices (curry, thyme, bay leaves)

Preparation Steps

  1. Blend the tomatoes, peppers, and onions together into a smooth puree.
  2. Boil the blended mix in a pot until the excess water evaporates.
  3. Heat vegetable oil in a large pot and fry sliced onions and tomato paste.
  4. Add the reduced pepper puree and fry thoroughly.
    1. Stir continuously to prevent burning at the bottom of the pot.
    2. Fry until the oil separates to the top of the tomato mix.
    3. Season with curry, thyme, salt, and seasoning cubes to taste.
  5. Pour in the seasoned chicken stock and bring to a rolling boil.
  6. Add the washed parboiled rice and stir to combine evenly with the stew.
  7. Cover the pot tightly with foil paper and a lid to trap the steam and cook on low heat.
  8. Allow the rice to absorb all liquid, then stir gently and serve hot.

4. Web Development Glossary

HTML
HyperText Markup Language, the standard language used to create and structure web pages.
The structural skeleton of any website interpreted by web browsers.
Browser
An application used to access and view websites on the internet (e.g., Chrome, Firefox, Safari).
URL
Uniform Resource Locator, the unique address of a resource on the internet.
Server
A computer program or hardware that serves up web pages and data to clients over a network.
Tag
A markup construct enclosed in angle brackets used to specify elements in HTML code.
Attribute
A modifier used inside an HTML tag to provide additional information or configure behavior.
Element
A complete component of an HTML document consisting of a start tag, content, and an end tag.
Accessibility
The practice of making web pages usable for people of all abilities and disabilities.
Markup
A system of symbols attached to a document specifying its structure and formatting.
Codebase
A system of symbols attached to a document specifying its structure and formatting.
